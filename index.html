Key Areas of Scrutiny

1. The "Executive Branch" Private Club

Launched April 2025

The Venture: Donald Trump Jr. co-founded "Executive Branch," an invite-only private club in Washington, D.C.'s Georgetown neighborhood, alongside conservative venture firm 1789 Capital.

The Scrutiny: The club reportedly charges a $500,000 founding membership fee (and up to $1 million for priority placement) while hosting Cabinet secretaries, White House officials, and corporate executives. Ethics critics have characterized the club as a mechanism for monetizing access to senior policymakers.

2. 1789 Capital Investments & Federal Awards

House & Senate Probes (2026)

The Venture: After Trump Jr. joined 1789 Capital Management, the firm expanded to over $3 billion in assets. Portfolio company Vulcan Elements saw a major valuation increase following a $620 million Defense Department loan and a $50 million Commerce Department grant, while Juul received FDA authorization for five products after 1789 Capital acquired a $5 million stake.

The Scrutiny: In August 2026, House Judiciary Committee Ranking Member Jamie Raskin opened an investigation into federal awards benefiting 1789 Capital portfolio firms. Separately, Senators Elizabeth Warren, Richard Blumenthal, and Andy Kim launched inquiries into Pentagon contracts and Persian Gulf sales involving drone firms such as Powerus.
